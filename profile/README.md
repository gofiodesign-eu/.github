# Hi there, this is gofiodesign.eu 👋

### Interdisciplinary Intervention Studio | Tenerife, Canary Islands 🇮🇨

We bridge the gap between **digital infrastructure** and **territorial sustainability**. Our studio integrates strategic branding and data networks with rewilding projects and social consultancy for energy efficiency.

---

### 🛠️ What we're currently up to
- 🔭 **Focus:** Digital systems integration, landscape architecture, and environmental mediation.
- 🌱 **Evolution:** Constantly refining our workflow with **Adobe Creative Suite** and deploying high-performance static sites via **Jekyll**.
- 👯 **Collaboration:** We are looking to collaborate on **Open Source projects** that empower communities through technology or sustainable territory management.

### 🧰 Tech & Tools
- **Design:** Brand Systems, Landscape Architecture, UX/UI, Heritage Interpretation.
- **Digital:** Telecommunications Infrastructure, Data Networks, Jekyll, HTML/CSS.
- **Impact:** Circular Logistics, Energy Efficiency Workshops, Social Innovation.

### ⚡ Fun Fact
"Gofio" is the heart of Canarian heritage—a traditional flour made from toasted grains. Much like this staple food, our studio takes raw, local elements and processes them into interdisciplinary solutions that sustain and grow the community.
